It is prepaid money that allows SSC to provide services like Money transfer, recharge, bill payments, travel booking, cash collection, etc. to their customers digitally

The Wallet can be loaded by depositing cash to SSC bank account or you can load through other digital modes like UPI, net banking, instant credit

To load your wallet through bank deposit mode follow the below steps:

Visit any SSC bank branch( bank account details are available in the bank deposit tab)

Deposit the cash into the bank account

Login to SSC App

Click on the bank deposit option available inside wallet recharge

Fill the required fields and submit the details of the payment made to the bank

The request will be sent to our internal team for review

Once the request is approved the wallet would be recharged instantly.

To load your SSC wallet through instant credit mode you have to add your virtual account number (available under Instant credit tab) as a beneficiary account in your bank account registered with SSC. Transfer the amount to the virtual account and the wallet will be loaded instantly.

To check your wallet load summary click on the 'Wallet history' tile then click on the 'Wallet load' tab your wallet summary would be visible you can also filter the summary basis status, date, mode, and transaction id

To load your SSC wallet through UPI follow the below steps

Enter the recharge amount

Click on the UPI tab

UPI enabled apps downloaded on your phone will be visible

Select one of the UPI apps

Collect request will be sent to the UPI app

Approve the request

The wallet will be loaded instantly
